# What's New

A short summary of the features added this week.

## 🗣 Multilingual Transcription + AI Summary
Live speech-to-text in **English, Hebrew, and Arabic** (with proper right-to-left
text), powered by Google Gemini. The host can also generate an AI summary of the
meeting. *Host-only, to save API quota.*

## 🤖 AI Meeting Assistant ("Ask AI")
A Quick Menu button for the host that answers questions about the meeting using
the live transcript — summary, action items, decisions, or any free-form question.

## ⏪ Catch Me Up
Players who join late get a one-tap AI recap of what they missed (key points,
decisions, open action items), generated by the host and sent just to them.
*Run `VRMP/Setup Catch Me Up` once to enable it in the scene.*

## 😀 Emoji Reactions
A "React" Quick Menu button that floats an emoji above your head for everyone to
see. Spam-throttled.

## 🎤 Podium Mode
The host can put the room into "podium" mode: everyone is seated and muted, and
can raise a hand to ask to speak. The host sees raised hands and can unmute people
one at a time.

## 🚪 Private Rooms (Breakout) — now isolated
Small breakout rooms (up to 4 people) you can invite others into. Members are now
**fully separated** from the main room: you only see and hear the people in your
own room, and chat stays within your room.

---

### How to set up (Unity Editor menu)
- `VRMP/Setup AI Assistant + Reactions`
- `VRMP/Setup Catch Me Up`
- `VRMP/Setup Podium Mode`

> Note: all of these need a 2-person test to confirm in a real session.
